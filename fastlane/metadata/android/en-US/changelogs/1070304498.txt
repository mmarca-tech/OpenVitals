Music controls on a Garmin watch: the watch shows what the phone is playing, and you can play, pause, skip, seek and change the volume from the wrist. Off by default; track details go only to the watch and are never stored. Older Garmin watches with no settings tree, such as the first Instinct, get up to ten alarms sent from the phone. "Send a point" is offered to more watches, the drink dialog no longer hides nutrients, and the new watch texts are translated.
