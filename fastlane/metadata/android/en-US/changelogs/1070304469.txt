CoMaps guidance now reaches a Garmin watch whenever CoMaps is navigating, on its own per-watch switch and no longer tied to recording an activity. A newly paired watch keeps its link to the phone by default, since weather, live readings, find-my-phone and guidance all need it. A caffeinated drink can be logged over the time it took, so a coffee spreads its caffeine instead of spiking. The app speaks French. Activities keep the title given on the watch, and long rides no longer fail to import.
