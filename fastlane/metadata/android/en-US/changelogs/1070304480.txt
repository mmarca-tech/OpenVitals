Workout plans: build a routine of exercises, rests and rounds and start it as a guided run - reps counted by the phone (push-ups and squats on the proximity sensor), timed steps and rests counting down on a ring, spoken cues. Plans live in Health Connect, so ones from other apps start too. Export any workout without its route as TCX, FIT or CSV. Every language is complete. A nap from a Garmin watch counts as sleep, and a night Mi Fitness stores twice no longer doubles its duration.
