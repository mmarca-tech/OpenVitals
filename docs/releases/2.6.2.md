# OpenVitals 2.6.2

Released 2026-08-08.

This release lets a reading into the app that no wearable took. HRV can now be logged by hand - for everyone whose RMSSD comes from a camera app or a chest-strap app rather than a watch - and every hand-logged vitals reading can finally carry the moment it was actually taken, not the moment it was typed in. Around that, a batch of dashboard and sleep fixes: day switching that answers the tap, tiles that never show another day's numbers, and nights that land on their own dates.

### Added

- **Manual HRV entry.** Heart rate variability joins the Log screen: one RMSSD value in ms, written to Health Connect as a manual entry, feeding the dashboard tile, the HRV screen, and baselines like any watch reading. Entries made in OpenVitals can be edited and deleted from the HRV day view; readings from other sources stay read-only. The tile sits in the Log grid's default layout and can be removed in its edit mode.

### Changed

- **Date and time pickers on every new vitals entry.** Blood pressure, SpO2, respiratory rate, body temperature, and HRV forms offered the pickers only while editing; a new entry silently stamped "now". They now show when adding too, defaulting to now, and reset after each save so the next reading defaults to now again.

### Fixed

- **Switching days on the dashboard answers instantly.** The date flips at once, tiles show a loading state instead of the previous day's numbers, and the rings sweep in exactly once with the right values - they used to animate twice, first with the old day's data.
- **The small weekly cardio tile no longer loads forever.** Placed alongside the cardio ring, it stayed on "Loading" even though its data was already on screen.
- **Coming back from a metric screen keeps your place.** The widget carousel returns to the page you were on and the dashboard keeps its scroll position, instead of jumping back to the first page through a loading flash.
- **No dot at the end of the hydration day graph.** Dots mark entries; the line's trailing hold at "now" is drawn as line only. Applies to every cumulative day chart (hydration, nutrition, mindfulness).
- **A sleep window set within one day files each night on its own date.** With a window like 00:00 to 12:00, the night window silently spanned 36 hours: each date showed whichever adjacent night was longer, whole nights appeared as naps, and the month view missed today. Midnight-spanning windows (the 18:00 to 10:00 default) were always correct and are unchanged.

The app keeps the same package name and signing certificate, so this installs as a normal update.

Distribution flow:

- Codeberg release with signed APK, signed debug APK, and signed Android App Bundle assets.
- Direct Google Play production upload from the approved Woodpecker deployment.
